Delete your TruckersPro account

TruckersPro by AIGodpro · How to delete your account and data

Option 1: In the app

Open TruckersPro, tap Account in the top-right corner of your dashboard, choose Delete account, and confirm. Your account and data are deleted right away.

Option 2: By email

Email admin@aigodpro.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We complete these requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your account is deleted together with every load, fuel entry, expense, pay schedule, and payment confirmation associated with it. Nothing is kept afterwards, and deleted data cannot be recovered.

Routine database backups are overwritten on a rolling 30-day cycle, so any copy in a backup is gone within 30 days.
